Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our business services

What is the minimum order quantity for bulk purchases?

We don't have a strict minimum order quantity. Whether you need 5 devices or 500, we can accommodate your business needs with competitive pricing.

What payment terms do you offer for businesses?

We offer flexible payment terms including NET 30, NET 60, and customized billing cycles based on your business requirements and credit approval.

Do you provide warranty coverage for business repairs?

Yes, all our business repairs come with a 90-day warranty on parts and labor. AMC customers receive extended warranty coverage as part of their contract.

What is your typical response time for business clients?

Business clients receive priority support with response times within 4 hours for critical issues and same-day service for most repairs. AMC customers get even faster response times.

Which areas do you service for business clients?

We primarily serve businesses in Bangalore and surrounding areas. For large contracts, we can arrange on-site support across Karnataka. Remote support is available nationwide.
